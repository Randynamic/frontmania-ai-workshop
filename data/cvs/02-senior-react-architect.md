## Summary

Front-end architect with twelve years of experience, the last five leading the
platform team of a sixty-person logistics scale-up. I own the Next.js
monorepo, the design system and the CI pipeline, and I review more code than
I write. Product has asked twice this year to put an AI assistant in the app;
my answer has been "not until we can measure it". I am at Frontmania to find
out whether anyone has an honest answer to that, and to take home patterns for
human-in-the-loop agents, tracing, and evaluation that survive a security
review.
