## Summary

Front-end engineer specialised in accessibility, working in the design-system
team of a mid-sized insurer for the past four years. I build and audit
components, write the accessibility acceptance criteria for product teams,
and test with screen readers every week. My recurring frustration is that
accessible components do not add up to an accessible application. I want to
know what modern CSS can take over from JavaScript for state and motion, and
what AI-generated UI does to the accessibility of the products we ship.
